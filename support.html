Add a widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then add Life Tik. To pick which countdown a widget shows, touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget. By default it shows your nearest countdown and moves to the next one when the day comes.

Retirement age

Choose where you'll retire. For mainland China and 11 other regions, the age comes from published state pension rules and is for reference only. You can always set the age yourself.

Apple Watch

Open Life Tik on your iPhone once after installing the watch app; your countdowns are sent to the watch.

Data

No account and no server. See the privacy policy.

问题反馈：zhang3917@hotmail.com，请写明 iPhone 型号和系统版本。

添加小组件

在桌面或锁屏长按，点「编辑」，添加「生命时钟」。要选显示哪个倒计时，长按小组件选「编辑小组件」。默认显示最近的目标，到期自动换下一个。

退休年龄

选择退休所在地。中国大陆和另外 11 个地区按公开的国家法定养老金规则计算，仅供参考；也可以自己设定年龄。

Apple Watch

装好手表 App 后，在 iPhone 上打开一次生命时钟，倒计时就会同步到手表。

数据

没有账号、没有服务器，详见隐私政策。
